PropOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE] · Data Protection Officer: [NAME], [EMAIL]

1. Who we are

PropOS ("we", "us") operates a property listing and technology platform. PropOS is a technology platform, not an estate agency. We comply with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 ("PDPA").

2. Personal data we collect

Account data (name, email, phone number, password hash); for agents, CEA registration number and the result of our check against the CEA Public Register; listing data you submit; enquiry and lead data (including when you tap a WhatsApp contact button); usage data (pages viewed, searches saved).

3. Purposes

We use personal data to: operate accounts and listings; connect enquirers with listers; verify agent registration against the official CEA register; generate AI marketing content you request; compute financial estimates you request; improve the service; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

4. Consent and marketing

We obtain consent at signup. We only send marketing messages with your separate opt-in, and every message includes an unsubscribe option. Singapore telephone numbers are checked against the Do Not Call Registry where required.

5. Disclosure

Data is disclosed only to: the counterparty you choose to contact (e.g. the lister you WhatsApp); service providers under contract (hosting, email, AI processing); and authorities where legally required. Each disclosure of financial profile data requires your explicit, per-recipient consent.

6. AI-generated content

Listing text you submit may be processed by AI providers to generate marketing content. AI-generated interior images, where offered, are watermarked and labelled as visualisations, not photographs.

7. Retention and security

Passwords are stored only as salted cryptographic hashes. Data is retained while your account is active and for up to [X] years after closure where required by law, then deleted or anonymised.

8. Your rights

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and withdraw consent, by contacting the DPO at [EMAIL]. We respond within the timelines required by the PDPA.

9. Complaints

Unresolved concerns may be directed to the Personal Data Protection Commission (pdpc.gov.sg).
