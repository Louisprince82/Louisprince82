# Revenue Model

Multiple compounding streams — never dependent on listing fees alone.

## Streams and sequencing

| Stream | Phase | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| Agent subscriptions | 1 | Free / Professional / Agency / Enterprise tiers. Free tier is the acquisition engine; AI quotas differentiate paid tiers. |
| Featured listings & placement | 1 | Sponsored slots in search & area pages; auction-priced. |
| AI marketing tools | 2 | Metered AI: video generation, staging, ad campaigns beyond tier quotas. |
| Mortgage referrals | 3 | Per funded loan from partner banks. High value in SG (large quantum). |
| Insurance & legal referrals | 3 | Per policy / per conveyancing file. |
| Renovation & vendor leads | 4 | Qualified-lead fee or % of contract via marketplace escrow. |
| Direct-factory commissions | 4 | Take rate on factory-direct orders (furniture, fittings). |
| Advertising | 4 | Brand campaigns from developers, banks, furnishers. |
| Analytics subscriptions | 5 | Market intelligence for agencies, developers, funds. |
| API licensing | 5 | Listings, area intelligence and finance APIs for third parties. |
| White-label platform | 5 | Full-stack licensing for agencies/developers; per-seat + revenue share. |
| Enterprise AI solutions | 5 | Custom AI deployments for large agencies and developers. |

## Unit-economics guardrails

- AI cost per listing pack is capped by the template fallback (zero-cost floor)
  and batched LLM calls (one call generates all channels — as implemented).
- Referral streams (mortgage, legal, insurance) carry near-zero marginal cost
  and monetize transactions the platform already hosts.
- Marketplace take rates only on escrowed, verified orders — trust is the moat
  ("Top N verified vendors"), not inventory breadth.
